# Voluntas Devina

## Install

The current update targets Earth Simulator 2.2.1, Steam build 26741176. Copy the `voluntas-devina` folder to the game's `mods` folder, enable it in Workshop and restart the game. Use the same mod files on both LAN computers. The pack includes its HUD and avatars.

## Start a match

Choose Voluntas Devina from the main menu. Set the seed, map size and terrain, confirm an avatar and create the world. The host's LAN address and seat count appear while waiting. The guest joins through Multiplayer and confirms an avatar before the match starts. Choose **Test match against a dummy** for a solo match. The dummy takes no player actions.

Continents, Pangaea and Islands are available at 25 × 25, 40 × 40 and 60 × 60, as well as the larger sizes. If the map cannot fit two capitals, setup reopens with your choices. Change the seed, size or terrain to try again.

Set your nickname in the profile card before hosting or joining. The match HUD shows both players' nicknames and your lifetime wins and losses. The totals are stored on this installation and carry over when you change your nickname. Completed matches between two players count toward the totals. Draws and dummy matches leave the totals unchanged.

Each player controls a god linked to one nation. The two nations start at war. Peace and the founding of additional nations are disabled during the match. The last surviving nation wins. If both fall in the same simulation tick, the match ends in a draw.

## Play

The top bar shows your mana out of 200, the current year, and your nation's era, population, GDP and active soldiers. Hover over or focus mana to see yearly income. In LAN, the host controls time.

Click the minimap to recenter the camera. The avatar beside it reacts to successful deeds and has a button for entering Walk at your capital. Use Walk's menu to return to the map. Click the handle at the right edge to open or close the map layers, or drag it to resize the panel. Change between 2D and 3D there or with the game's shortcut, Tab by default. Each living nation's capital carries its color on a large flag in 3D.

You enter your capital in first person on a cloud. Use the Walk movement keys to fly at up to 10 km/h, or hold Shift for up to 100 km/h. Hold Space to rise or Ctrl to lower the cloud. Press V to start landing and steer toward a dry, gently sloping surface. Once you land, V switches the camera again. Tap Space twice quickly from the ground to call the cloud back.

Your incarnation has 1,000 HP. Use the left mouse button for fists and Q for Lightning. Aim at a target within 12 metres. Lightning deals 300 damage before protection and takes 60 real seconds to recharge, even on a miss. Ordinary weapons are unavailable to incarnations.

Damage prevents a return to the map for 20 real seconds from the last hit. If your incarnation dies, you can pan and zoom the map, switch between 2D and 3D, and use the map layers while you wait. After 60 real seconds your match controls return and you can enter Walk again. Active timers are carried in solo saves and LAN room state.

| Benevolence | Mana at default and maximum | Malevolence | Mana at default and maximum |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Rain | 5–18 | Hurricane | 20–40 |
| Animals | 8–28 | Flood | 24–48 |
| Resources | 12–30 | Earthquake | 30–60 |
| Inspiration | 18–36 | Meteorite | 45–90 |

Select a deed to see its controls and mana price. Move the pointer over the map to preview the target. A red outline marks an unavailable target or insufficient mana. Click a tile to attempt the deed. Each attempt clears the selection. Only an effect that takes place spends mana.

Rain and Animals have brush size and strength controls. Resources uses brush size. Disaster strength sets its radius. Inspiration gives a boost to research and starts a golden age in your nation. Aim it at one of your nation's tiles.

Release aurochs on land to support hides and leather production where the biome allows it. Release cod in the sea to support fishing at nearby populated coasts. Wolves live on land and yield trophies in Walk. The game keeps up to 40 active disasters, replacing the oldest when a new one reaches that limit.

Click a regiment of your nation to select it. The Battles layer shows regiments in 2D. Choose **Attack** and click an enemy border tile that the regiment can reach by marching through your territory. Your avatar marks the selected regiment. Supply, terrain and the strength of the armies determine the outcome. Artillery provides support under the game's automatic orders. You can replace a target or cancel the order. A capture, a blocked route or loss of the regiment ends its order.

Solo saves keep the selected avatar, mana, nations and orders. Active orders travel with the LAN match state when a player reconnects. Compatible 0.1.0 saves receive a default avatar and start the match war if needed. A save with additional living nations needs 0.1.0 or a new match under the 0.2.0 rules.
